What the test does

  • Reloads the page as a phone or a desktop and scrolls down it (up to 60 s); on an article it stops at the article's end.
  • Measures the ads the way Chrome does and checks the Better Ads limits.
  • Stay on this tab while it runs: in another tab or window the test pauses.

Measuring

Stay on this tab until the test ends: in another tab or window it pauses. Until then, the page ignores your scrolling, clicks and typing. Chrome shows a “started debugging this browser” bar; Cancel there also stops the test.

What do these give you?
  • Open full size: the whole page in the view on screen (Real or Chrome), in a new tab.
  • Open comparison: one image with both views of this test side by side, under the page's full URL: on the left what Chrome tags, on the right what a reviewer would count.

  • Download comparison: the comparison as a JPG.

Test values use Chrome's ad detection and density rules; they can differ from DevTools by about 2 points. Ad CPU and network: CrUX only.

Chrome sets no thresholds for its ad metrics; the Better Ads limits are the only documented ones.
